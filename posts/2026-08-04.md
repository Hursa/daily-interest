# Something Fun Today — August 4, 2026

A champion speedcuber just reclaimed the Guinness World Record for solving a Rubik's Cube blindfolded, clocking in at 11.56 seconds. The trick is that all the solving happens purely from memory: the cuber studies the scrambled cube first, then puts on the blindfold and works through the entire sequence using nothing but what they memorized. It's a fun reminder that a 50-year-old puzzle toy still has room for humans to push the limits of speed and memory.
